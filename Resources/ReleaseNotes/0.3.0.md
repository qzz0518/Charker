# Charker 0.3.0

Save more than one 160W, and Charker connects to whichever one is nearby.

## New

- Every 160W you connect is saved under Devices & Connection › My Chargers. Moving between places no longer means forgetting one and scanning again.
- Give each charger its own name. It shows in the sidebar and the menu bar popover.
- When two are in range, switch from My Chargers or from the menu bar icon’s right-click menu.
- Add Charger connects another one without forgetting the current charger.

## Fixes

- The nearby device list can be scanned while a saved charger is out of range.
- Connecting to another device in the nearby list now disconnects the current charger first.
- After switching chargers, the power chart and energy history no longer continue the previous charger’s readings.
