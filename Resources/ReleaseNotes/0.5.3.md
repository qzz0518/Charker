# Charker 0.5.3

## Improvements

- The main window can be made shorter; the overview scrolls when it doesn't fit.
- Schedule times have stepper arrows, and a display-hours end time typed as 24 becomes 23:59.
- Compact Spacing in Menu Bar settings puts the reading closer to the icon.

## Fixes

- With Show Icon in Dock off, the main window was left out of Stage Manager and Command-Tab. The Dock icon now shows while the window is open and hides once it closes.
