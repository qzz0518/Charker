# Charker 0.5.6

## Improvements

- On macOS 26 and later, the sidebar uses the system style.
- Segmented options such as the energy range and appearance slide when you switch.
- Dialogs accept Return to confirm and Esc to cancel.

## Fixes

- The 3D model and page content no longer jitter sideways when you switch pages.
- In a narrow window, port labels in the 3D view no longer wrap onto two lines.
