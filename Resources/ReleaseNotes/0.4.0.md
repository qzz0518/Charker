# Charker 0.4.0

## New

- Control the 250W: port output, countdowns and on/off schedules, charging modes and priority ports, custom charging modes.
- Change the 250W's brightness, screen timeout, clock screensaver and theme, including your own pictures.
- 250W Labs switches: maximum compatibility and device recognition.
- The 160W gains fixed allocation and custom charging mode, and countdowns can be cancelled.
- The 160W shows the model of recognised devices on each port.

## Improvements

- The window opens just large enough for the overview, with no scrolling or resizing.
