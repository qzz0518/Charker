新功能

  • 支持保存多台 160W 并切换，到哪里自动连上身边那台。
  • 每台可以单独起名。

Version 0.2.1

Build 3 · September 20, 2026

Fixes sign-in succeeding but finding no charger for some accounts outside mainland China.

  • Accounts in Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, South Korea and several other regions were sent to the wrong Anker server and saw no devices after signing in. They now reach the right one.
  • If the country you pick differs from where the account was registered, Charker switches to the account's own server.
  • If you are already signed in and still see no devices, sign out and sign in again.

Version 0.2.1

Build 3 · 2026年9月20日

修复部分海外账号登录成功却找不到充电器的问题。

  • 香港、台湾、新加坡、韩国等地区的账号之前连错了 Anker 服务器，登录后提示账号下没有设备，现在已改对。
  • 登录时选的国家和账号注册地不一致时，自动改用账号所在的服务器。
  • 已经登录、仍然找不到设备的账号，退出后重新登录一次。

Version 0.2.0

Build 2 · September 14, 2026

Adds support for the Anker Prime Charger 250W and phone login for mainland China accounts.

  • Anker Prime Charger 250W (A2345): sign in with the Anker account the charger is bound to and see live power and energy history for all six ports. Read-only for now; requires macOS 15 or later.
  • Select China to sign in with your phone number and an SMS code. Other regions still use email and password.
  • On first launch, pick your charger model or try the simulated device.
  • Drag to rotate and scroll to zoom the 250W 3D model.
  • Set a manual range for the power chart, and click a port to show only its curve.
  • Menu bar readings and energy history now cover six ports.

Version 0.2.0

Build 2 · 2026年9月14日

新增 Anker Prime 充电器 250W 支持，国行账号可以用手机号登录。

  • 支持 Anker Prime 充电器 250W（A2345）：登录绑定设备的 Anker 账号，查看六个端口的实时功率和能耗记录。目前只读，需要 macOS 15 或更高版本。
  • 选择中国大陆后，用手机号和短信验证码登录；其他地区继续用邮箱和密码。
  • 首次打开先选充电器型号，也可以直接体验模拟设备。
  • 250W 的三维模型可以拖动旋转、滚轮缩放。
  • 功率图可以手动设置量程，点端口只看这一口的曲线。
  • 菜单栏读数和能耗记录支持六个端口。

Version 0.1.0

Build 1 · August 31, 2026

The first public release: live power and energy history for the Anker Prime 160W on your Mac.

  • Live voltage, current, power, cable and charging protocol for C1, C2 and C3, with a 3D model and a power timeline.
  • Energy history by session, today, this week, this month or all time, with electricity cost estimates, scoped clearing and CSV / JSON export.
  • Customizable menu bar readings; turn ports on and off, set shutdown timers, and change display language, brightness, auto-lock, orientation and auto-rotation.
  • First-connection guidance, and chargers are remembered and reconnected.
  • Try it without hardware using the simulated charger, kept apart from real history.
  • Notarized by Apple, with in-app updates.

Version 0.1.0

Build 1 · 2026年8月31日

第一个公开版本：在 Mac 上看 Anker Prime 160W 的实时功率和能耗记录。

  • 实时查看 C1、C2、C3 的电压、电流、功率、线材和充电协议，配合三维模型和功率曲线。
  • 能耗记录可以按本次、今天、本周、本月或全部历史查看，支持电价估算、分范围清空和导出 CSV / JSON。
  • 菜单栏读数可以自定义；可以开关端口、设定倒计时，调整屏幕语言、亮度、自动锁屏、方向和自动旋转。
  • 首次连接有指引，连过的充电器会记住并自动重连。
  • 没有充电器也能用模拟设备体验，模拟数据不会混进真实记录。
  • 应用经过 Apple 公证，支持应用内更新。
